## 1. CUBE (Campus Utility, Built for Everyone)
**Kind:** College companion app · **Year:** 2026 [you confirm] · **Role:** Design and build
**Live:** cu-be.vercel.app · **Code:** github.com/Peru-107/Timetable [you confirm it can be public]

**One line:** Your whole college day in one free app.

**The problem:** A student's day is spread across a timetable photo, an attendance sheet, a grades portal and a group chat that breaks the moment campus Wi-Fi blocks it.

**What it does:**
- Scan your timetable once; it becomes your schedule
- Mark classes with a swipe and see exactly how many hours you can still miss
- Grades with SGPA and CGPA on any scale (4, 10 or your own)
- Study notebook that turns notes into quizzes and flashcards
- Campus map to find your room
- Nearby chat with classmates that works with no internet

**Story:** CUBE grew out of **Attendly**, my first version (timetable, attendance, grades, notes and exam quizzes, offline). CUBE added the campus map and offline nearby chat.

## 2. Nextup
**Kind:** Web app + Chrome extension · **Year:** 2026 · **Role:** Design and build [you confirm]

**One line:** Turn what you save into what you do.

**The problem:** We save posts, links and screenshots to "do later", and later never comes.

**What it does:**
- Save by link, screenshot or the Chrome extension; each save gets **one** next action
- A short list for the week (This week, Later, Archive, Done) with Undo everywhere
- Free and Pro plans, with UPI payments approved from an admin panel
- 7 styles, 12 palettes (all checked for contrast), 11 ambient backgrounds

**Built with:** Next.js, TypeScript, Chrome extension (Manifest V3), SQLite and Postgres tested.
**Process note:** planned through a "war room" of seven AI agents debating the product, then built from an approved design preview.

**Visuals:** the product card image + one of the ready-made reels (cursor demo).

## 3. Folio
**Kind:** Reading and study app (installable, works offline) · **Year:** 2026 · **Role:** Design and build

**One line:** Read a little every day.

**What it does:**
- A Today page in one calm editorial column
- A focus room with a timer (25, 45 or open), breaks and a full-screen focus mode
- A sound mixer: 9 procedurally generated channels, 6 presets, save your own
- **Sproutling**, a live SVG character that breathes, blinks, follows your eyes, reads with you in focus mode and grows (Seed → Sprout → Sapling → Bloom → Grove) as you read

**Craft notes:** redesigned from five competing UI styles into one "Clothbound" system with day and night themes; custom line icon set; ~400 KB first load, layout shift 0; 64-screenshot review at phone and desktop sizes.

**Visuals:** live screenshots of the app at phone size + the Sproutling character.

## 4. Glass Home (TV launcher)
**Kind:** Android TV home screen · **Year:** 2026 · **Role:** Design and build

**One line:** A calm glass home screen for the TV, built for a remote and a weak GPU.

**What it does:**
- Rotating nature wallpapers, one frosted dock of app tiles, a full-screen glass "All apps" sheet
- Focus that springs and lifts with a white ring, readable from 3 m away
- Clock, wallpaper, night and profile panels; music controls

**Craft notes:** designed for a remote control only (D-pad); only cheap effects (moving and fading things, pre-blurred images) because TV chips are slow; written in plain Java (~4,200 lines) and built with a script, no Android Studio; installed on my own TV.

**Visuals:** the real screenshots from the TV (home, focus states, panels) in a TV frame.

## 5. byperraz engine
**Kind:** Content tool for my Instagram, @byperraz · **Year:** 2026 · **Role:** Design and build

**One line:** A small studio that runs on my laptop and makes design-tip carousels.

**What it does:**
- Plans a 30-day calendar of design and front-end tips
- Writes each carousel, renders 4:5 slides from an HTML template, writes captions and alt text
- A dashboard where I preview everything, and nothing is posted until I click Approve
- Stages posts at 13:00 and 18:00 and sends a Windows notification

**Built with:** Python, Flask, Playwright, Gemini.

**Visuals:** real carousel slides (the "One Job Per Color" palette post) shown as a stack + the dashboard.
